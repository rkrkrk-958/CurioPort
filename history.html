History

How a stretch of coastline and kampong grew into today's Katong-Joo Chiat.

Home / History

A Timeline of the Neighbourhood

Late 1800s

Coastal Beginnings

The area was once seaside land dotted with villas, popular with wealthy families seeking a retreat by the water.

Early 1900s

Peranakan Settlement

Peranakan families settled in, building terrace houses decorated with ornate tiles, timber shutters, and pastel facades.

Mid 1900s

A Growing Community

Shophouses filled in along the main roads, mixing residences with trades, tailors, and kitchens that are still spoken of today.

Today

A Living Heritage District

Katong-Joo Chiat is now a conserved heritage area, balancing old shopfronts with a new generation of cafes and studios.
