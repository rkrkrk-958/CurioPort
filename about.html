Why We Built CurioPort

Katong Joo Chiat isn't just a place on a map; it’s a tapestry of flavors, colors, and histories waiting to be uncovered. CurioPort transforms the area into an interactive canvas for exploration.

We provide the compass—via our digital platform and neighborhood interactive booths—but YOU create YOUR journey. Grab your adventure passport, hunt down local hidden gems, and design your own keepsake with custom-illustrated sticker stamps of the iconic sights and bites you discover along the way. Whether you follow our curated highlights or drift off the beaten path, you’re in control of how you connect with the heart and soul of this historic district.
