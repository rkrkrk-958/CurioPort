Why We Built CurioPort

CurioPort started as a small idea between neighbours who wanted visitors to see Katong-Joo Chiat the way locals do — one shopfront, one story, and one plate of food at a time. What began as a weekend walking route has grown into a full self-guided trail.

Our Mission

Help every visitor slow down and notice the details that make this neighbourhood one of a kind.

Community First

We work directly with shop owners and residents so the trail reflects real stories, not just landmarks.

The Team

A small group of heritage enthusiasts, designers, and residents who grew up in the area.
