About CurioPort

Collect memories. Craft your adventure.

Katong-Joo Chiat has plenty on offer, from its lively shophouse streets to its layered mix of communities and heritage. Our trail is designed to take you beyond the surface, guiding you toward the small details that make this neighbourhood one of a kind.

We provide the compass — you set the pace and explore in your own order. Grab your adventure passport, and design a journey of hidden corners and neighbourhood landmarks. The trail is filled with stops both familiar and new to first-time explorers.
