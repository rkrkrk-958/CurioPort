What to Look Out For

Every facade along the trail tells you something about when it was built and who lived there. Here's what to notice as you walk.

Painted Tiles

Colourful ceramic tiles imported in the early 1900s, often floral or geometric, framing doors and windows.

Timber Shutters

Louvred wooden shutters that let air through while keeping the tropical sun out.

Five-Foot Way

The covered walkway in front of every shophouse row, a sheltered path connecting one shop to the next.

Ornamented Pediments

Decorative crests above the upper floor, often carrying a family name or the year the building was completed.
