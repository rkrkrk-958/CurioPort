Off the Main Road

These are the stops most visitors miss on their first walk through. Tucked-away corners worth slowing down for.

Cat Socrates

A beloved independent lifestyle store in Joo Chiat, known for its thoughtfully curated collection of books, stationery, home décor, plants, gifts, and accessories from both local and international designers. With its cozy atmosphere and iconic resident cat, Cat Socrates offers a charming shopping experience that reflects the vibrant character of the Katong and Joo Chiat neighbourhood.

East Coast Comedy Stand-Up

One of Singapore’s leading live comedy showcases, featuring a rotating lineup of established local comedians alongside rising talent. Known for its lively atmosphere and fresh performances, each show offers a unique mix of observational humor, witty storytelling, and crowd interaction, ensuring that no two nights are the same.

La Tienda

A charming lifestyle boutique located in the heart of Joo Chiat, offering a carefully curated collection of fashion, home décor, accessories, and artisanal products inspired by Mediterranean living and Peranakan heritage. Housed in a traditional heritage shophouse, the store provides a relaxed, personalized shopping experience where visitors can discover unique, authentic pieces that celebrate craftsmanship, culture, and timeless design.
