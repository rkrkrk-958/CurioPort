Koon Seng Road Shophouses

Koon Seng Road's iconic pastel-colored shophouses were built in the 1920s and were once home to wealthy Peranakan and Eurasian families. The area was designated a Conservation Area by the Urban Redevelopment Authority (URA) in 1991, protecting its historic facades, timber windows, and decorative tiles. Today, these privately owned homes continue to serve as residences while preserving Singapore's Peranakan heritage and attracting visitors interested in Katong's rich history.

Katong Shopping Centre

Opened in 1973, Katong Shopping Centre was one of Singapore's earliest fully air-conditioned shopping malls and was regarded as a modern landmark of its time. It offered a comfortable indoor shopping experience when traditional shophouses and open-air markets were still the norm. Today, despite the redevelopment of the surrounding area, the mall remains open and continues to preserve its distinctive 1970s architecture, original terrazzo flooring, and nostalgic atmosphere, making it a beloved landmark that reflects Katong's rich history and heritage.

Joo Chiat Police Station

The police station was built in 1928 by the Singapore Improvement Trust/Public Works Department to serve the growing Joo Chiat and Katong communities. It played an important role in maintaining law and order during a period when the area faced problems with secret societies, smuggling, and illegal gambling. The station was decommissioned in the late 1980s as policing was reorganized into larger regional headquarters and Neighborhood Police Centres. Recognized for its historical and architectural value, the main building was conserved and later incorporated into Katong Square in the 2010s. Today, it has been repurposed into a dining destination, housing establishments such as Baba Chews Bar & Eatery and a heritage-themed Starbucks.
